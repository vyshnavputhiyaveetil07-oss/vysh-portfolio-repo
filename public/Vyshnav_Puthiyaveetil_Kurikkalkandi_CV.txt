• Built a 3-page Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, regional profit heatmaps, discount scatter plots and drill-through audit tables, using DAX measures for YTD, YoY and rolling 3-month figures.

• Set out three commercial actions (discount caps, courier renegotiation and marketing budget reallocation) worth an estimated £140,000+ in recovered annual margin.
